==English==

===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*pleḱ-}}
Borrowed from {{bor|en|fr|déployer||to unroll, unfold}}, from {{der|en|fro|desploiier}}, itself from {{m|fro|des-}} + {{m|fro|ploiier}}, or possibly from {{der|en|la-lat|displicāre||to unfold, display}}, from {{der|en|la|dis-||apart}} + {{m|la|plicare||to fold}}. Compare {{cog|enm|desployen,dysployen|t=to unfold, display}}. {{doublet|en|display}}.
