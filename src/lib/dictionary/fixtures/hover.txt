{{root|en|ine-pro|*keh₂p-}}
The {{glossary|verb}} is derived from {{inh|en|enm|hoveren|t=to float in the air, hover; to stay}},<ref>{{R:MED Online|entry=họ̄veren|pos=v|id=MED21406}}</ref> probably from {{m|enm|hoven|t=hover; of a bird: to fly high in the air, soar}}<ref>{{R:MED Online|entry=họ̄ven|pos=''v.''(1)|noformat=1|id=MED21401}}</ref> (which it displaced) + {{m|enm|-er-|pos={{glossary|frequentative}} {{glossary|suffix}}}}.<ref>{{R:MED Online|entry=-er-|pos=''suf.''(3)|noformat=1|id=MED14274}}</ref> {{m|enm||Hoven}} is probably derived from {{inh|en|ang|*hōfian}}, from {{m|ang|hōfon}}, the {{glossary|plural}} {{glossary|past}} {{glossary|indicative}} form of {{m|ang|hebban|t=to lift, raise}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*habbjan}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*habjaną|t=to lift; to heave}}, ultimately from {{inh|en|ine-pro|*kap-|*keh₂p-|to hold, seize}}. The English word is analysable as {{suffix|en|hove|t1=(''obsolete'') to remain suspended, float, hover; to linger, wait|er|id2=frequentative|pos2=frequentative suffix}}.<ref>Compare {{R:OED Online|pos=''v.''{{sup|1}}|noformat=1|id=88986|date=December 2022|nodot=1}}; {{R:Lexico|pos=v}}</ref>

The {{glossary|noun}} is derived from the verb.<ref>{{R:OED Online|pos=''n.''{{sup|2}}|noformat=1|id=88984|date=June 2022|nodot=1}}; {{R:Lexico|pos=n}}</ref>
